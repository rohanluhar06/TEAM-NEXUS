Fake Account Red Flags

High Risk

Sudden Follower Spikes

Gaining thousands of followers overnight or in unrealistic timeframes

Very Low Engagement

High follower count but minimal likes, comments, or shares

Repetitive Content & Comments

Generic comments like "Great post!" on many unrelated posts

Authentic Account Indicators

Low Risk

Steady Follower Growth

Organic growth over time with normal fluctuations

Healthy Engagement Rate

3-6% engagement rate with meaningful comments

Diverse, Original Content

Varied posts with personal touches and authentic interactions
